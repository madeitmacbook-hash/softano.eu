Condition

What new, pre-owned and refurbished mean at Softano.

Every Softano product carries a clear condition label. Software as New or Pre-Owned, hardware as New or Refurbished — legally documented and fully functional in every case.

Software

Licences for Windows, Server, SQL & Office

New
First-hand licences direct from authorised sources. Never previously activated, full manufacturer term, perpetual licence without subscription.
Pre-Owned
Used volume licences from verified prior ownership. Fully usable, complete chain of title, transfer legally documented.

Hardware

Servers, storage & infrastructure

New
Factory-new systems with the full manufacturer warranty — off the shelf as a ready configuration, or built to your specification.
Refurbished
Professionally reconditioned and function-tested, configured to your specification on request. Grades A+ and A only, drives erased to certified standards.

Condition grades for refurbished hardware

A+As newNo visible signs of use. Visually indistinguishable from new goods.
AVery goodIsolated minimal marks on the chassis, barely noticeable at normal viewing distance.

Both grades pass identical technical testing — the grading is purely cosmetic.

Trading in used software is permitted within the EU (Sec. 69c No. 3 German Copyright Act; CJEU judgment C-128/11, „UsedSoft“). Every pre-owned licence is supplied with a complete chain of title. Refurbished hardware includes a data erasure record.
